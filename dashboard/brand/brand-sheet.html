Usage & Voice
  • Numbers are the hero. Every price, P&L, and weight uses the mono face with tabular numerals so columns align. Prices never reflow.
  • Neon is punctuation, not paint. The cyan→emerald gradient marks identity and the single most important value on screen — not every border.
  • Red/green mean money only. Profit/loss colors are reserved for P&L. Status uses its own palette (emerald-running, red-halt, amber-stale).
  • Always say PRACTICE. The demo-account badge is permanent chrome. AXIOM never implies real money.
  • Honest empty states. No data → an explicit "not connected / awaiting first cycle" state. Never a placeholder number that could read as real.
  • Buddy is the engine; AXIOM is the terminal. Buddy acts, AXIOM observes — read-only, always.
