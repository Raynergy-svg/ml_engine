REGIME CLASSIFICATION (4 levels)

  0 - LOW              ATR < 25th percentile (avoid trading)
  1 - NORMAL           ATR 25-75th percentile (normal)
  2 - HIGH             ATR 75-95th percentile (good conditions)
  3 - EXTREME          ATR > 95th percentile (caution)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

STRATEGY HINTS (4 types)

  MOMENTUM            Trend > 0.55 AND ADX > 25 → Use momentum strategy
                      Risk Multiplier: 1.0x

  MEAN_REVERSION      Trend < 0.45 AND ADX < 20 → Use counter-trend
                      Risk Multiplier: 0.8x

  RANGE               Uncertain/Transition → Use range trading
                      Risk Multiplier: 0.7x

  CAUTION             Changepoint Alert + HIGH vol → Reduce exposure
                      Risk Multiplier: 0.5x

ALGORITHMS

  1. BOCPD (Adams & MacKay 2007)    → Changepoint detection
  2. Hurst Exponent (R/S Analysis)  → Trend classification
  3. ADX (Wilder 1978)              → Trend strength
  4. Volatility Clustering          → Regime buckets
  5. Ensemble Consensus             → Final decision
